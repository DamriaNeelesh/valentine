💍 PROMISE DAY

11 Waade – 11 Ehsaas

(11-11 ke naam)

🌱 PROMISE 1 — Understanding

Main yeh wada karta hoon,
jab tum chup ho, main yeh nahi sochunga ki tumhe farq nahi padta,
main yeh samjhunga ki tum mehsoos kar rahi ho.

🌸 PROMISE 2 — Apnapan

Main promise karta hoon,
tumhe kabhi yeh feel nahi hone dunga ki tum akeli ho,
tum meri apni ho — aur hamesha rahogi.

🌊 PROMISE 3 — Emotional Safety

Jab tum hurt ho,
main tumhe strong banne ko nahi kahunga,
main bas tumhare saath baith jaunga.

☁️ PROMISE 4 — Khamoshi

Main yeh wada karta hoon,
tumhari khamoshi se darrunga nahi,
main usse samajhne ki koshish karunga.

🔥 PROMISE 5 — Respect

Chahe baat chhoti ho ya badi,
tumhari feelings meri nazar mein
hamesha important rahengi.

🌙 PROMISE 6 — Growth

Main perfect hone ka wada nahi karta,
par har din thoda better banne ka wada karta hoon —
tumhare liye.

🌼 PROMISE 7 — Happiness

Jab tum hanso,
main uss hansi ki wajah banna chahta hoon,
aur jab udaasi aaye, main kahin nahi jaunga.

🕊️ PROMISE 8 — Distance

Chahe hum door ho,
main yeh wada karta hoon ki mera yakeen,
kabhi door nahi hoga.

🤍 PROMISE 9 — Friendship

Main sirf tumhara husband nahi,
tumhara dost bhi ban ke rehna chahta hoon —
bina judgement ke.

🌹 PROMISE 10 — Sukoon

Main yeh wada karta hoon,
tumhara sukoon meri zimmedari hoga,
chahe zindagi jaisi bhi ho.

✨ PROMISE 11 — 11-11

11 November ko humne ek-dusre ko choose kiya tha,
main aaj yeh wada karta hoon,
har din, har haal mein, main tumhe hi choose karta rahunga.

💗 Forever

Yeh waade sirf aaj ke liye nahi,
yeh meri zindagi ka tareeqa hain — tumhare saath.

Main tumhe har din, har haal mein, phir se choose karunga.
Happy Promise Day, Sana❤️.
